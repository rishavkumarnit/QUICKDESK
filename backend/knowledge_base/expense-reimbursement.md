# Expense reimbursement requests

This is a fictional QuickDesk demonstration policy. Employees submit business expenses through the finance expense portal. Create a claim with the purchase date, amount, currency, business purpose, and applicable expense category. Attach an itemized receipt in that portal and send the claim to your manager for approval. Avoid putting full payment card or bank account details into a helpdesk ticket.

Finance reviews approved claims for completeness and policy compliance before arranging reimbursement. Submission alone does not guarantee approval or a payment date. If a receipt is missing, contact Finance through a ticket and explain the circumstances; do not create a replacement receipt or assume an exception will be granted.

For a delayed claim, include the claim reference, submission date, and current portal status in your Finance ticket. Support can route the query to the finance team but cannot promise reimbursement timing or authorize an expense. Correct any rejected claim according to the reason displayed in the portal before resubmitting it.
