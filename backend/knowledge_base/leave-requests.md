# Applying for leave

This is a fictional QuickDesk demonstration policy. Employees submit planned leave through the company's HR portal. Open the leave section, choose the appropriate leave type, enter the start and end dates, and review the remaining balance shown in the portal. Include a short handover note for work that needs coverage during the absence. Submit the request to your reporting manager for approval.

A submitted request is not automatically approved. Check the portal for its approval status before treating the dates as confirmed. If the balance appears incorrect, open an HR ticket with the leave type and dates involved. Do not include sensitive medical details in a general support ticket.

For an unexpected absence, notify your manager as soon as practical and record the request in the portal when you are able. HR can clarify eligibility and documentation requirements. Support agents cannot approve leave on a manager's behalf or change an employee's recorded leave balance without the authorized HR process.
