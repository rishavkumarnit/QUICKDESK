# VPN connection troubleshooting

This is a fictional QuickDesk demonstration policy. Employees use the company VPN client installed on their managed laptop. Before troubleshooting, confirm that normal internet access works by opening a public website. Disconnect any personal VPN software, then close and reopen the company VPN client. Do not change server addresses or disable security software.

If the problem began after a company password change, sign out of the VPN client completely and sign in using the new company password. Complete the multifactor authentication prompt. Never send your password, recovery codes, or one-time codes to support. If a saved credential continues to fail, restart the laptop and try signing in once more.

If the connection still fails, provide the exact error message, your operating system, and the approximate time of the failure in the ticket. Support will investigate account access and client configuration. Do not repeatedly approve unexpected authentication prompts; report those to IT immediately.
